Methodology

Computed.Not guessed.

01

The address register

Your address is resolved against the state’s official register to one certain lot and plan — an exact match, or we come back to you. The analysis is never built on a geocoder’s best guess.

02

Cadastre & overlays

Land, dimensions, zoning, precinct — and every planning overlay tested against your parcel’s actual polygon. Affected, partial or clear: each result is dated and printed in the report.

03

Rate card

A current construction and civil-works rate card maintained from real SEQ building experience. Costing the build is the rare part.

04

Comparable evidence

End values anchored to recent, verified sales near your property — the same evidence a valuer relies on. The model never invents a sale price.

05

Simulation

Scenario stress plus 10,000 simulated combinations of cost and value movement — the verdict comes with its full P10 / P50 / P90 range, not a single hopeful number.

06

The final read

The computation is the start, not the verdict. Every report is read, challenged and signed off by a person before it reaches you.

Overlay audits run automatically for Logan · Brisbane · Gold Coast · Moreton Bay — everywhere else in Australia we check by hand. Same report, same evidence.
