Privacy
policy.

Effective 26 July 2026 · Australia

Who we are

Tayloe Capital (“we”, “us”) operates tayloecapital.com.au. We provide property feasibility analysis (the Hidden Equity Report), digital research products, and an email newsletter. You can reach us at tayloecapital@gmail.com or through the enquiry form on the Hidden Equity page.

What we collect

  • Waitlist & newsletter — your email address.
  • Enquiries — your email address and the property address you ask about.
  • Store purchases — handled by Squarespace and its payment providers. We never see or store your card details.
  • Analytics — basic page-view statistics collected by Squarespace’s built-in analytics.

How we use it

To answer your enquiry and prepare your report; to send you the newsletter you signed up for (every email includes an unsubscribe link); and to deliver products you purchase. We do not sell or rent personal information.

Where it lives

Enquiry and subscriber records are stored with Google (Google Sheets); newsletters are sent through Brevo; the site and store run on Squarespace. These providers may store data on servers located outside Australia.

Your choices

Unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email. To access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, email us and we will action it promptly. Enquiry records are kept only while relevant to providing the service.

The law

We handle personal information consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles (Privacy Act 1988 (Cth)) and the Spam Act 2003 (Cth). If you have a complaint, contact us first; you may also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
